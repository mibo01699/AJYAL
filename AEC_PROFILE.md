# Arabian Eagle Corporation (A.E.C.) – Profile

## 🦅 Who We Are

**Arabian Eagle Corporation (A.E.C.)** is a technology company specializing in research, development, and consulting in Web3 blockchain systems and decentralized infrastructure.

**Founded:** January 1, 2024  
**Sector:** Blockchain, Web3 Infrastructure, Fintech  
**License:** Open Source (MIT)  

## 🎯 Mission & Vision

- **Mission:** To build open-source Web3 solutions, financial technology, and digital public goods that promote financial inclusion and empower youth and women in conflict-affected regions.
- **Vision:** To establish a decentralized parallel economy model, starting with Yemen as a proof of concept, then scaling globally.
