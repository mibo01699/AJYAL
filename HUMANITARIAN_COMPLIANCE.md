# 🛡️ Humanitarian Compliance & Community Data Privacy Protocol
## Framework: Conflict Zone Survey Ingestion & Dashboard Synchronization (Yemen 2026)

This protocol governs the open-source field-intelligence gathering framework within the integrated blockchain environment, satisfying international data protection standards (GDPR / ICRC Data Protection Rules) inside conflict-affected regions.

---

### 1. Data Anonymization & Cryptographic Masking
To isolate sensitive socio-economic and living condition indicators from physical identities, the application strictly separates individual Pi KYC tokens from community survey answers:
* **Zero PII Leakage:** Names, exact wallet addresses, and biometrics are completely stripped from the analytical payload at the client-side level before transmission.
* **Geographical Generalization:** High-precision GPS tracking is disabled. Field intelligence relies strictly on broad conflict-zone matrices (`zone_id`) to mitigate localized security risks.

### 2. Anti-Corruption & Dual-Layer Audit Trails
To enforce absolute administrative integrity and eliminate internal fraud, nepotism, or double-dipping:
* **One-Time Access Link Isolation:** Remote administrative boards cannot be accessed via persistent static passwords. All terminal connections require a one-time cryptographic link generated via an enterprise mail server integrated with an automated operational manager.
* **Hardware Fingerprinting:** Every administrative session logs the specific workstation hardware data. Unverified devices attempting remote data injection are instantly locked out.
* **Immutable Voucher Linkage:** Humanitarian aid distribution inside `AJYAL` is cross-verified in real-time with daily academic engagement metrics and calculated via zero-float `BigInt` formulas to prevent the generation of ghost beneficiaries.

### 3. Financial Crime & Liquidity Sanction Safeguards
All conversions executed directly through the **Pi DEX Liquidity Pools** (converting Pi to regional stablecoins `YER` for salary or merchant payment settlement) pass through an automated AML/CFT monitoring ledger. 
* Any automated pool purchases deviating from certified merchant profiles are auto-suspended.
* Financial data is immutably anchored to local sovereign nodes to guarantee transaction transparency and support real-time forensic auditing by authorized international funding bodies.
