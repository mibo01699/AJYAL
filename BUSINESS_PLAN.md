# 📈 AJYAL Business & Sustainability Plan (Sandbox/Testnet)

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## 1. Value Proposition & Market Need

Traditional educational platforms (e.g., Coursera, edX) rely on stable internet, international banking, and high bandwidth, making them inaccessible to millions in conflict zones.

**AJYAL** solves this by providing a lightweight, decentralized platform that:
- Operates natively within the Pi Browser.
- Eliminates the need for traditional banking infrastructure.
- Introduces a **Learn-to-Earn (L2E)** framework to fight local poverty.
- Uses geographical ring-fencing to protect resources.

---

## 2. Sustainability & Monetization Streams

AJYAL operates as a **Digital Public Good (DPG)** under the MIT License for end-users. Sustainability is achieved through the following mechanisms:

| Mechanism | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **B2B & NGO Ingestion Fees** | International NGOs and corporations can host custom vocational training pipelines for a flat integration fee. |
| **Verification & Attestation Commissions** | Employers pay a micro-transaction fee to verify the authenticity of a graduate's credentials via the `SovereignScholarshipHub.js`. |
| **Anonymized Telemetry Dashboards** | Subscription-based access for researchers and UN agencies to aggregate, anonymized educational and economic data. |
| **Clearing Settlement Fees** | A marginal protocol-level fee on voucher conversions executed via the `GAV` merchant network. |

---

## 3. Cost Structure

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Infrastructural Overhead** | Cloud hosting (Replit), API load-balancing, protocol compliance, and core maintenance. |
| **Student Incentive Escrow** | Direct funding streams allocated to smart escrows for student micro-grants and special-needs vouchers. |

---

## 4. Strategic Roadmap (2026-2027)

| Phase | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **Phase 1: Prototype Validation** | Deploy open-source backend, participate in ecosystem hackathons, and seek early-stage DPG certification. |
| **Phase 2: Sandbox Deployment** | Launch pilot in Yemen, targeting youth and women, and onboard the first merchant terminals via `GAV`. |
| **Phase 3: Global Scaling** | Expand to adjacent conflict zones and establish formal credential recognition with regional universities. |

---

## 5. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Currency Volatility** | Use of `BigInt` fixed-point arithmetic tethered to stable crypto-assets (Pi Network) and cleared via the `BIGISH-YER` treasury. |
| **Sybil Attacks / Fraud** | Mandatory biometric verification via the Pi Authentication SDK to prevent duplicate profiles. |
